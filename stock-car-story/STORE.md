## Full description (Play: 4000 chars max)

```
Pop left you a rusty garage, one tired stock car and a rookie driver. Ten
years later, will your name be on the championship trophy?

STOCK CAR STORY is a pixel-art racing team management sim. You never touch the
wheel — you build the team that wins.

RUN THE SHOP
· Hire from 36 drivers and 23 crew chiefs, each with their own strengths
· Train drivers across 15 programmes — from a morning jog to drift school
· Level up your crew: their Tech builds better cars and turns faster pit stops

BUILD THE MACHINE
· Research 15 chassis, from a Street Stocker to the NextGen Cup car
· Fit 30 parts across engines, tyres, gearboxes, aero, brakes and pit gear
· Upgrade permanently — every level you develop carries into New Game+

RACE 16 REAL TRACK SHAPES
· Flat half-mile paperclips, high-banked concrete bowls, slick dirt ovals
· 1.5-mile quad-ovals, egg-shaped speedways, a three-cornered triangle
· 2.5-mile tri-oval superspeedways where the draft decides everything
· A proper road course with esses and a hairpin

REAL RACING, SIMULATED
· Qualifying sets your grid
· Drafting pulls you along the straights on the big tracks
· Tyres go off, fuel runs down, and the crew calls you to pit road
· Caution flags bunch the field — and on the superspeedways, watch for the
  Big One
· Stage points at a third and two thirds distance, then a playoff finale

BUILD THE BUSINESS
· Sign sponsors, fill their ad gauges, unlock exclusive parts and training
· Grow a fanbase that brings bigger names to your door
· Win a championship to earn a bigger garage, more crew and a second team

Spend your auras wisely: one charge can supercharge a training session, a car
build, a part install, an upgrade — or give you a shot of speed on the last lap.

No ads. No timers. No internet needed. Your save lives on your device.
```

## Privacy policy

You need a public URL for both stores. This text is sufficient and accurate:

```
Stock Car Story — Privacy Policy

Stock Car Story does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

The game stores your progress (career save and developed machine/part library)
locally on your device using standard browser storage. This data never leaves
your device and is removed if you delete the app or clear its data.

The game contains no advertising, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and
requires no network connection to play.

Contact: <your email>
Last updated: <date>
```

If you later add ads or in-app purchases, this must be rewritten to name the
SDK, what it collects, and link its policy — and both stores' data-safety
declarations must be updated to match.

## Legal note

Every name in the game is original. Tracks, sponsors, drivers and teams are
invented parodies (Colonel Cluck's, Mocha Cola, Yellowhammer Super); no real
series, sanctioning body, team, driver or trademark is referenced, and all art
is generated in code. Keep it that way in future updates: naming a real series
or using real team liveries would need a licence, and store takedowns for that
are quick and expensive to reverse.
