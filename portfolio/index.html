Sheet 02 · Fieldwork

Toronto, six cities deep

An interactive map of Toronto's six former municipalities — the boroughs that amalgamated in 1998 — built end-to-end: open data sourced, geometry validated in code, rendered and styled in Kepler.gl via Dekart. Live and explorable, not a screenshot of someone else's work.

Pipeline

Boundary geometries pulled from the City of Toronto Open Data CKAN API, reprojected source verified as WGS84, reduced to a clean layer of six MultiPolygons with attributes.

Validation

Every polygon area computed programmatically before mapping: 641.9 km² total against the city's official ~630 km² land area — Scarborough 188, North York 177, Etobicoke 126, Old Toronto 106.

Cartography

Qualitative ColorBrewer Set2 palette on a dark basemap, hairline borders, ordinal color scale by borough, tooltips carrying name and area — styled for legibility at city scale.
