Sheet 02 · Fieldwork

Three studies of one city

Built end-to-end with a modern geospatial stack — open data pulled by API, spatial SQL in DuckDB (H3 indexing, projected-CRS nearest-neighbour joins, point-in-polygon aggregation), every result validated in code against ground truth, then rendered in Kepler.gl via Dekart. Each map is live and explorable, each README on the report documents method, findings, and caveats — the same discipline a screening-level constraints memo demands.

Fig. 1 · Density & equity

The urban forest, tree by tree

All 688,335 city-managed street trees (304 species) aggregated to H3 resolution-9 hexagons — color and height encode canopy density, and the dark canyons are the ravine system reading itself out of the data. A point-in-polygon join against 158 neighbourhoods computes trees/km² and basal area per hectare, exposing a 6× equity gap between Forest Hill South (2,470 trees/km²) and Thorncliffe Park (416).

Fig. 2 · Infrastructure gap

EV charging deserts

Every expressway and arterial segment in Toronto — 10,591 segments, 1,505 km — scored by distance to its nearest public charger via a nearest-neighbour join computed in projected UTM 17N metres. Cool cyan hugs the charger clusters downtown; the hot orange deserts concentrate in Scarborough, where Kingston Rd and St Clair E peak at 5.5 km from any mapped station. 38% of the arterial network sits more than 2 km out.

Fig. 3 · Regulatory screening

The permit screener

The three spatial layers that most often trigger environmental approvals in Toronto — the Ravine & Natural Feature Protection Area (854 polygons; a permit trigger under Municipal Code Ch. 658), 92 Environmentally Significant Areas, and 320 km of watercourses buffered to a 30 m screening setback in projected metres — unioned and quantified per neighbourhood. 18% of the city’s land carries at least one permit trigger. The computed ravine area (111.0 km²) independently reproduces the city’s published ~11,000 ha figure, and the exposure choropleth flags Morningside Heights at 70% constrained.
